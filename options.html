Sticky Fullscreen

Extension Enabled Turn Sticky Fullscreen on or off without removing it.
Show Fullscreen Button Toggle whether the small "Enter Fullscreen" button is shown on allowed sites. The button automatically hides after 10 seconds.
Only After Redirect/Navigation When enabled, the extension will not trigger fullscreen on the initial page load — it will only perform fullscreen after a subsequent navigation or redirect.
Hide Mouse Cursor When enabled, the mouse cursor will automatically hide after 10 seconds of inactivity on allowed sites.
Overlay Placement Choose where the fullscreen overlay button appears. Center placement can be convenient in Arc after redirects.
Auto-Exit Fullscreen Leave fullscreen when navigating away from allowed sites or when switching away from a fullscreen tab.
Site Rules One rule per line. Format: pattern [overlay=on|off] [cursor=on|off]. Use * as a wildcard or start with re: for regex. Order matters.
Allowed Domains (Legacy) Optional legacy allowlist. This is kept for compatibility and can be left empty.
